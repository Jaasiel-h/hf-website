Our process

We guide your capital through a transparent process that validates risks, structures sound transactions and maintains visibility into every project.

  1. 1

    Comprehensive assessment

    We analyze each borrower's credit risk, validate the transaction's cash flow and verify the collateral securing the financing.

  2. 2

    Tailored structure

    We define rights, obligations and covenants, establishing clear contracts and trust vehicles that protect invested capital.

  3. 3

    Ongoing monitoring

    We oversee each transaction with monthly reports, conduct site visits and trigger early warnings on deviations or defaults.

  4. 4

    Strategic diversification

    We diversify the portfolio by sector, term and region to balance risk and return, reinvesting proceeds according to your wealth objectives.
